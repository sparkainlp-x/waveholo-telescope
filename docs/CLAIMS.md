# Claim policy

Claims require every gate: protocol and configuration identified; complete seeds and
required metrics; uncertainty and failure fraction reported; provenance and checksums
verified; synthetic scope explicit; and no conclusion beyond the measurement.

## Allowed only after benchmark completion

- “Under synthetic condition X, residual RMS wavefront error was A ± B.”
- “Under synthetic condition X, focus error was A ± B.”
- “Recovery failed beyond aberration amplitude X under noise condition Y.”
- “The benchmark identifies a sampling threshold below which Zernike mode recovery
  becomes unstable.”

## Forbidden

- “WaveHolo Telescope recovers telescope wavefronts.”
- “WaveHolo Telescope works on real telescope data.”
- “WaveHolo Telescope validates adaptive optics.”
- “WaveHolo Telescope models atmospheric turbulence.”
- “WaveHolo Telescope achieves diffraction-limited correction.”
- “WaveHolo Telescope is flight-ready or observatory-ready.”

No claim gate authorizes physical telescope or real-atmosphere claims. Negative
controls and failed conditions remain in the result bundle.
